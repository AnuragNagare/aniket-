# Work Trail Privacy Notice

**Version:** 2026-10-v1  
**Last updated:** October 2026

This notice explains how Work Trail collects, uses, and protects personal data during your work hours.

---

### 1. What we collect
Location (latitude, longitude, accuracy, speed, battery level) about every 5 minutes; the start and end times of work; the updates you write or speak.

### 2. When
Only between **Start work** and **End work**. Never when you are off duty. A notification is always visible in your status bar while tracking is on.

### 3. Why
So your company can see work hours, routes between work locations, and your reports.

### 4. Who can see it
Admins of your company. Work Trail staff only for technical support, when your company requests it. AI summaries are generated from your update text only. Your location is never sent to the AI service.

### 5. How long
Precise locations are deleted after the company's retention period (default 90 days, configurable between 30 and 365 days). Work hours and updates are kept as your company's work record until erasure is requested.

### 6. Your choices
- Tap **End work** at any time to stop tracking.
- Turn off location permissions in your Android settings.
- Request your company to delete your data from **Profile → Privacy**.
